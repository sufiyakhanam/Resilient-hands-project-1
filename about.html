Our Story & Mission

A journey of care, respect, and bringing peace to the lives of our revered elders.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, respectful, and nurturing environment for abandoned and vulnerable senior citizens. We ensure they receive the healthcare, nutrition, and emotional companionship they need to live independently with unwavering dignity.

Our Vision

A society where every elder, regardless of their circumstances, is embraced with love, given the respect they deserve, and empowered to enjoy a beautiful and peaceful twilight filled with joy.

Our Journey

2015

Sri Sairam Old Age Home was established in a small rented house in Chennai, welcoming our first 5 elders into a supportive family setting.

2018

Moved to our permanent campus. We opened our in-house medical clinic with specialized care for age-related illnesses and consistent health monitoring.

2024

Expanded facilities to comfortably shelter over 85 senior citizens, complete with beautiful meditation gardens, physiotherapy hubs, and recreational spaces.
