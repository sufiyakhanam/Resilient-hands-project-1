About Sri Sai Ram Adharavu Illam

Sri Sai Ram Adharavu Illam was established in 2021 by Mrs. M.P. Rajeswari with the vision of providing care, compassion, dignity, and emotional support to senior citizens.

The organization believes that every elderly individual deserves respect, love, and a peaceful environment. The home provides care, companionship, and support while ensuring that every resident lives with comfort and dignity.

Founded on the values of compassion, service, and humanity, Sri Sai Ram Adharavu Illam continues to serve senior citizens with dedication.

Mission

To provide love, care, dignity, and emotional support to elderly individuals.

Vision

To build a society where every senior citizen lives with respect, happiness, and security.
