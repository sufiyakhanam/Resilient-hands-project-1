Stories of Wisdom

Meet the wonderful residents whose lives mirror decades of history and enduring grace.

Rukmini, 82

Rukmini was a classical singer. Although her family is far away, she brings light to our halls with her evening prayers and gentle melodies.

Sponsor Rukmini's Care

Narasimhan, 78

A true intellectual at heart. He once worked as a professor and now finds comfort in reading the morning newspaper and debating current events.

Sponsor Narasimhan's Care

Savithri, 85

Savithri is our resident storyteller. She loves recounting tales of Old Madras to the volunteers and enjoys her evening tea in the garden.

Sponsor Savithri's Care

Govind, 72

Always full of energy despite his age, Govind loves tending to our community plants. His confident smile brightens the entire home.

Sponsor Govind's Care

Saraswathi, 89

Saraswathi is peaceful and profoundly observant. She found solace here after the tragic loss of her immediate family, and is deeply loved.

Sponsor Saraswathi's Care

Kannan, 76

A retired railway worker with a booming laugh. Kannan enjoys organizing small chess tournaments with the younger volunteers every weekend.

Sponsor Kannan's Care
